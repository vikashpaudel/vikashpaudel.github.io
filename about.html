A Trusted Name in Dermatology

Professional Overview

Dr. Vikash Paudel is a highly qualified and experienced consultant dermatologist and venereologist practising in Kathmandu, Nepal. With extensive training in both medical and aesthetic dermatology, Dr. Paudel brings a comprehensive approach to skin care, combining clinical expertise with the latest advancements in dermatological treatments.

Currently serving as a consultant dermatologist and associate professor, he is committed to providing accessible, high-quality dermatological care to patients across the Kathmandu Valley, and — through teledermatology — to patients in different parts of the world.

Professional Philosophy

Dr. Paudel believes in a holistic approach to dermatological care, combining evidence-based medical treatments with personalised care plans. His practice philosophy emphasises patient education, preventive care, and the use of the latest technologies to achieve optimal outcomes. He is committed to staying at the forefront of dermatological advancements through continuous learning and professional development, ensuring patients receive world-class care.
