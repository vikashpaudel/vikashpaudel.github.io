Official Professional Biography

Dr. Vikash Paudel

Senior Consultant Dermatologist, Venereologist & Associate Professor

Dr. Vikash Paudel is a Nepal Medical Council–registered Senior Consultant Dermatologist and Venereologist (NMC #9111) and Associate Professor of Dermatology. With more than a decade of specialist clinical experience, he provides evidence-based medical, pediatric, surgical, and aesthetic dermatology care as well as confidential sexual health consultations across Kathmandu, Lalitpur, Dhangadhi, and via online teledermatology.

Patient-Centred Care

Clinical Practice

Dr. Paudel’s clinical practice centers on accurate diagnosis, rational prescription, and clear patient counseling for skin, hair, nail, and mucosal conditions across all age groups. He emphasizes evidence-based medical management and discourages the misuse of over-the-counter steroid combinations and unverified cosmetic procedures.

Acne, Pigmentation & Medical Dermatology

Comprehensive evaluation of adolescent and adult acne, post-acne hyperpigmentation, and acne scarring, alongside chronic inflammatory and infectious dermatoses including eczema, psoriasis, urticaria, vitiligo, melasma, fungal infections, and pediatric skin conditions.

Explore Acne & Scar Consultations

Hair Loss & Scalp Consultations

Diagnostic workup (including trichoscopy/dermoscopy) and targeted medical or procedural therapy for androgenetic alopecia (male and female pattern thinning), telogen effluvium, alopecia areata, seborrheic dermatitis, and scarring alopecias.

Explore Hair & Scalp Services

Venereology & Confidential Sexual Health

Discreet, non-judgmental specialist consultations for sexually transmitted infections (STIs), genital dermatoses, preventive screening, and partner management guidelines conducted with strict patient privacy.

Confidential STI & Venereology Care

Dermatosurgery, Laser & Teledermatology

Outpatient skin biopsies, mole and cyst excision, nail surgery, electrocautery, cryotherapy, chemical peels, and laser procedures—alongside structured online teledermatology consultations for patients across Nepal and abroad.

Clinical Scholarship & Health Literacy

Research, Publications, and Patient Education

Dr. Paudel has authored and co-authored more than 25 peer-reviewed research articles, clinical studies, and case reports in national and international dermatology journals. His research investigates practical clinical challenges affecting patients in South Asia.

  • Core Research Interests: Epidemiology and clinical management of acne vulgaris, pigmentary disorders (melasma and vitiligo), topical corticosteroid misuse on the face, superficial fungal infections, pediatric dermatoses, leprosy, sexually transmitted infections, dermoscopy, and teledermatology delivery in Nepal.
  • Selected Publications & Academic Repository: His published studies cover clinico-epidemiological profiles of pediatric and adult skin diseases, quality-of-life assessments in chronic dermatoses, and therapeutic evaluations in tertiary hospital settings. Browse the complete, indexed bibliography and verified academic profile links on the Research & Publications page.

Public Health Literacy & Patient Education

Treating patient education as an essential pillar of clinical care, Dr. Paudel writes bilingual (Nepali and English) health guides, newspaper columns in The Kathmandu Post and Onlinekhabar, and scripts educational videos to dispel common skin myths, seasonal skin damage, and STI stigma.

Academic Service, Journals & Societies

Editorial Roles, Recognition, and Professional Affiliations

Beyond clinical practice, Dr. Paudel serves in editorial leadership and peer-review roles for medical journals and actively participates in national and international dermatology conferences.

  • Editorial Leadership: Serves in editorial leadership as Editor-in-Chief of the Nepal Journal of Dermatology, Venereology and Leprology (NJDVL), the official scientific publication of SODVELON.
  • Invited Journal Peer Reviewer: Performs peer reviews for international and national indexed journals, including the Journal of the European Academy of Dermatology and Venereology (JEADV) and the Journal of Patan Academy of Health Sciences (JPAHS).
  • Conference Presentations & Recognition: Regular faculty speaker, session chair, and award recipient at national and international dermatology congresses (including SODVELONCON). View full citations on the Awards & Recognition page.
  • National Registration & Life Memberships: Permanent Specialist Registration with the Nepal Medical Council (NMC #9111); Life Member of the Society of Dermatologists, Venereologists and Leprologists of Nepal (SODVELON) and the Nepal Medical Association (NMA).
  • International Society Affiliations: Member/Fellow of the European Academy of Dermatology and Venereology (EADV), International Dermoscopy Society (IDS), International Society of Teledermatology (ISTD), South Asian Regional Association of Dermatologists (SARAD), and Associate Member of the European Academy of Allergy and Clinical Immunology (EAACI).
