Common Skin Diseases: Symptoms, Causes, Treatment and Prevention

Skin diseases affect people of all ages and impact physical comfort, confidence, and quality of life. Recognizing early symptoms, avoiding unsupervised steroid creams, and consulting a dermatologist helps achieve safe, effective outcomes.

Acne & FacialNon-contagious

Acne Vulgaris & Acne Scars

Comedonal, Inflammatory & Nodulocystic Acne · Also Rosacea & Perioral Dermatitis

Clogged hair follicles causing blackheads, whiteheads, red bumps, or deep painful nodules on face, chest, and back. Early dermatologist care prevents permanent scarring.

Clinical Details

Care: Gentle non-comedogenic cleanser, prescribed topical retinoids/benzoyl peroxide, or oral therapy for severe acne. Avoid squeezing pimples or applying steroid/fairness creams.

Fungal InfectionsContagious

Tinea (Ringworm) & Fungal Infections

Tinea Corporis, Cruris, Pedis, Capitis · Pityriasis Versicolor · Candidiasis (दाद)

Itchy, circular or irregular scaly patches common in Nepal's warm humidity. Avoid over-the-counter steroid combination creams, which cause steroid-modified tinea (tinea incognito) and resistant infections (Trichophyton indotineae).

Clinical Details

Care: Complete prescribed antifungal therapy, keep skin folds dry, wear loose cotton clothes, avoid sharing towels, and treat affected household members. Never take unsupervised oral antifungals.

Bacterial InfectionsVariable

Cellulitis, Impetigo, Folliculitis & Boils

Furuncles, Carbuncles, Erysipelas & Erythrasma

Bacterial infections ranging from honey-crusted sores (impetigo) and painful boils to spreading redness, warmth, and swelling (cellulitis). Timely targeted antibiotics prevent complications.

Clinical Details

Warning: Seek urgent medical evaluation for rapidly spreading painful redness, fever, or diabetic foot infections.

Bacterial InfectionsCurable (Low infectivity)

Leprosy (Hansen Disease)

Treatable Chronic Mycobacterial Infection (कुष्ठरोग)

A completely curable disease. Early signs include persistent pale or reddish skin patches with reduced sensation to touch or heat, or numbness in hands and feet. Not spread by casual touch.

Clinical Details

Care: Early Multi-Drug Therapy (MDT) cures the infection and prevents nerve disability.

Consult
Viral InfectionsContagious

Warts, Molluscum, Herpes & Shingles

Common/Plantar Warts (मुसा) · Herpes Zoster (जनै खटिरा) · HFMD

Viral skin conditions causing rough growths (warts), pearly dimpled bumps (molluscum), or painful grouped blisters (herpes simplex, shingles). Early antiviral care for shingles reduces nerve pain.

Clinical Details

Urgent sign: Painful shingles affecting the forehead, nose, or eye requires prompt medical evaluation.

ParasiticContagious

Scabies, Lice & Insect Bite Reactions

Mite Infestation (लुतो) · Pediculosis · Papular Urticaria · Cutaneous Larva Migrans

Scabies causes intense nighttime itching between fingers, wrists, waist, and groin, often affecting multiple family members. Simultaneous treatment of close contacts prevents reinfection.

Clinical Details

Care: Wash bedding/clothes in hot water; note that mild post-scabetic itch can linger 2–4 weeks after treatment.

Atopic Dermatitis & Eczema

Contact Dermatitis · Hand Eczema · Nummular & Seborrheic Dermatitis

Chronic dry, itchy, inflamed skin driven by skin-barrier dysfunction and environmental triggers. Regular fragrance-free moisturizing and supervised therapy keep flare-ups controlled.

Note: Not every itchy rash is caused by food allergy; indiscriminate allergy blood panels are rarely helpful.

Urticaria (Hives) & Angioedema

Symptomatic Dermographism · Deep Lip/Eyelid Swelling (डाबर / छालाको एलर्जी)

Raised, itchy wheals that appear and fade within 24 hours, sometimes with deeper swelling. Seek emergency care immediately if accompanied by breathing difficulty or tongue/throat swelling.

Clinical Details

Care: Evidence-based non-sedating antihistamines and structured evaluation for chronic urticaria (>6 weeks).

Psoriasis, Lichen Planus & Pityriasis Rosea

Silvery Scaly Plaques (कत्लेरोग / सोरायसिस) · Violaceous Papules

Non-contagious immune-mediated conditions causing well-defined scaly plaques on elbows, knees, and scalp (psoriasis) or itchy purplish bumps (lichen planus).

Clinical Details

Care: Topical therapy, phototherapy, or systemic/biologic options; screen for joint pain.

PigmentationNon-contagious

Melasma & Hyperpigmentation

Chaya Poto (चाया पोतो) · PIH · Freckles · LPP · Acanthosis Nigricans

Patchy facial darkening driven by UV/visible light, hormones, and inflammation. Daily broad-spectrum or tinted sunscreen is essential; avoid fairness/bleaching creams and unsupervised steroid mixes.

Clinical Details

Care: Evidence-based depigmenting topicals and procedural options when appropriate.

PigmentationNon-contagious

Vitiligo & Pityriasis Alba

White Skin Patches (दुबी)

Vitiligo is a non-contagious autoimmune loss of skin pigment; pityriasis alba causes faint scaly pale patches on children's cheeks. Not every white patch is vitiligo.

Clinical Details

Care: Topical immunomodulators, narrowband UVB phototherapy, and sun protection.

Hair & ScalpMostly Non-contagious

Hair Loss & Scalp Disorders

Pattern Hair Loss · Telogen Effluvium · Alopecia Areata · Dandruff (कपाल झर्ने समस्या)

Hair thinning or shedding may stem from genetics, post-illness stress, thyroid/iron imbalance, autoimmune patches, tight hairstyles, or scalp fungal infections.

Clinical Details

Care: Trichoscopy and targeted evaluation; avoid unproven miracle oils or supplements.

NailsVariable

Nail Disorders & Onychomycosis

Fungal Nails · Nail Psoriasis · Paronychia · Ingrown Toenails · Melanonychia

Not all thickened or discolored nails are fungal—nail psoriasis and shoe trauma look similar. New, irregular dark vertical nail bands (melanonychia) need prompt medical review.

Clinical Details

Care: Confirm fungal diagnosis before oral therapy; protect hands during wet work.

ChildhoodCondition-dependent

Childhood & Infant Skin Diseases

Diaper Dermatitis · Cradle Cap · Childhood Eczema · Birthmarks · Hemangioma

Children's delicate skin absorbs topical medicines rapidly. Never use adult steroid creams or adult doses on infants or children without specialist guidance.

Clinical Details

Care: Gentle barrier creams, age-appropriate dosing, and early review of growing hemangiomas.

Bullous Pemphigoid · Pemphigus Vulgaris · Cutaneous Lupus

Immune-mediated disorders causing persistent blisters, painful mouth erosions, or sun-sensitive rashes. Early biopsy-confirmed specialist care is essential.

Clinical Details

Safety: Never self-medicate with systemic corticosteroids or immunosuppressants.

Respectful, confidential evaluation for genital ulcers, warts, or itching. Not every genital lesion is an STI—eczema, fungal rashes, and normal variants are also common.

Skin Tags · Cysts · Keloids · Actinic Keratosis · BCC · SCC · Melanoma

While most moles, cysts, and skin tags are benign, any new, changing, bleeding, or non-healing spot (watch ABCDE: Asymmetry, Border, Colour, Diameter, Evolution) needs in-person dermoscopy.

Dry Kathmandu winters trigger xerosis and chilblains, while humid Terai summers and high-altitude UV provoke prickly heat, sunburn, and seasonal fungal flare-ups.

Patient Safety

When Should You Seek Medical Attention?

Routine Dermatology Visit

  • Persistent itching, acne, or rashes lasting >2 weeks
  • Recurrent fungal infections or hair/nail changes
  • New or slowly changing moles and growths

Urgent Medical Assessment

  • Rapidly spreading painful redness with fever
  • New rash after starting a medication
  • Painful shingles near the eye, forehead, or nose

Immediate Emergency Care

  • Difficulty breathing or wheezing
  • Swelling of face, lips, tongue, or throat
  • Widespread blistering, skin peeling, or mouth/eye erosions

Call 102 (Nepal) or visit the nearest hospital emergency immediately.
