Patient Support Initiative

Why Your Contribution Matters

This initiative supports patients experiencing genuine financial hardship who cannot afford essential dermatological evaluation and medical treatment.

How Donation Funds Are Utilized

  • ✓ Essential dermatological consultations for financially disadvantaged patients.
  • ✓ Necessary prescription medications.
  • ✓ Basic laboratory investigations and diagnostic procedures.
  • ✓ Essential dermatological treatment and minor medical procedures.
  • ✓ Treatment of chronic and disabling skin diseases.
  • ✓ Support for patients affected by leprosy, severe inflammatory skin diseases and neglected dermatological conditions.
  • ✓ Essential follow-up care where clinically indicated.

Strict Fund Exclusion Policy

Donations are never used for personal expenses, elective cosmetic or aesthetic procedures, commercial activities, or unrelated expenditure.

Fair & Non-Discriminatory Selection

Patient selection is based strictly on clinical necessity and verified financial vulnerability, without discrimination based on gender, ethnicity, religion, caste, geographical location, or social background.

Patient Dignity & Confidentiality

Beneficiary names, photographs, and clinical diagnoses are never displayed publicly for fundraising. Public expenditure records use anonymized patient codes only.

Patient Support Request

Need Medical Support?

Patients experiencing genuine financial hardship who are unable to afford essential dermatological care may confidentially request support below.

Privacy Protection: Sensitive medical records or financial documents are collected only during confidential clinical review—never through open web uploads.

Important Application Guidelines

  • 1 All applications are reviewed strictly in confidence.
  • 2 Assistance is determined by genuine financial need, clinical assessment, and available initiative funds.
  • 3 Submitting an application does not guarantee financial assistance.

Medical Emergency Notice

This support initiative does not provide emergency response services. Patients experiencing acute or life-threatening medical emergencies should visit the nearest hospital emergency department immediately.

Medical Ethics & Governance

Our Commitment to Ethical Patient Care

“This initiative is established to help financially disadvantaged patients access essential medical care. Contributions are entirely voluntary and do not influence clinical decision-making, appointment priority or the quality of care provided to any patient. All verified donations and expenditures will be publicly documented. Patient dignity, medical confidentiality and responsible utilization of funds will remain central to this initiative.”

Governance & Regulatory Transparency: This initiative operates as a transparent patient care support program and is not represented as a registered charity, NGO, foundation, or tax-exempt entity unless formally registered and documented. Tax exemption certificates or statutory audit reports are not promised unless supported by verified official documentation.
