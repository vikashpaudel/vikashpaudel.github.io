Research Interests

Core clinical and public-health themes across Dr. Paudel’s hospital studies, surveys, and systematic reviews.

Teledermatology in Nepal

Evaluating adoption patterns, diagnostic utility, and barriers to mobile and asynchronous teledermatology in rural and resource-limited healthcare settings.

Dermatophytosis & Antifungal Resistance

Investigating the rising burden of chronic superficial fungal infections in southern Nepal, antifungal susceptibility patterns, and topical corticosteroid misuse.

Chronic Urticaria & Allergic Dermatoses

Studying clinical triggers, patch-test reactivity in contact dermatitis, and long-term symptom control in chronic urticaria and inflammatory skin disorders.

Leprosy in the Post-Elimination Era

Documenting clinico-epidemiological, histopathological, and socioeconomic profiles of leprosy—including atypical presentations—along the Nepal–India border.

Sexually Transmitted Infections & Genital Dermatoses

Examining STI epidemiology, non-venereal genital skin conditions, and venereophobia to improve accurate diagnosis and reduce stigma.

Leprosy Panorama Across the Nepal–India Border in the Post-Elimination Era

Key finding: Tertiary-center cross-sectional analysis characterizing ongoing multibacillary cases, reaction states, and diagnostic delays in border communities despite national elimination status.

Our Dermatol Online · DOI: 10.7241/ourd.20243.4
Published (2021) Dermatophytosis & Antifungals

Clinico-Mycological Study of Dermatophytosis and Antifungal Susceptibility

Key finding: Hospital-based mycological evaluation identifying predominant dermatophyte species and variable antifungal susceptibility linked to chronic, recalcitrant tinea infections.

NJDVL · DOI: 10.3126/njdvl.v19i1.34693
Published (2021–2022) STIs & Genital Dermatoses

Non-Venereal Genital Dermatoses & Venereophobia in Hospital Outpatients

Key finding: Demonstrated that a substantial proportion of patients presenting with genital skin concerns have non-sexually transmitted dermatoses or venereophobia requiring reassurance and accurate diagnosis.

Our Dermatol Online · DOI: 10.7241/ourd.20221.3
Ongoing Clinical Registry Chronic Urticaria

Clinical Course & Treatment Response in Chronic Spontaneous Urticaria

Focus: Tracking symptom severity, antihistamine step-up response, and quality-of-life outcomes among adult outpatients with chronic urticaria in Kathmandu and Lalitpur.
