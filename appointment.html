with Dr. Vikash Paudel - Consultant Dermatologist

Dr. Vikash Paudel is a board-certified Consultant Dermatologist with over 10 years of experience in medical, surgical, and aesthetic dermatology. He specializes in acne treatment, skin surgery, hair disorders, and pigmentation issues. Committed to evidence-based care, he practices at Elite Health Clinic (Lagankhel), Shankhamul Healthcare, and Shubharambha Skin Centre (Dhangadhi), and offers teledermatology services daily.
