Patient Information

Frequently Asked Questions

What conditions does Dr. Vikash Paudel treat?

Dr. Paudel diagnoses and treats acne, eczema, melasma, psoriasis, vitiligo, fungal infections, hair loss, and sexually transmitted infections (STIs), and performs dermatosurgery and cosmetic procedures including Botox, fillers, chemical peels, and LASER therapies.

How can I book an appointment with Dr. Paudel?

You can book directly via the 'Book Now' button on this website, submit the Quick Slot Request form above, message via WhatsApp (+977-9825207253), or call Shankhamul Health Care at +977-1-5242511.

Where are the clinic locations?

Dr. Paudel practices at Shankhamul Health Care (Kathmandu), Elite Health Clinic (Mahalaxmisthan, Lalitpur), Patan Hospital (PAHS, Lalitpur), and Shubharambha Skin Centre (Chatakpur, Dhangadhi).

Does Dr. Paudel offer teleconsultation?

Yes, teledermatology consultations are available by prior arrangement via Zoom or WhatsApp for follow-ups, international patients, and non-emergency skin concerns.
